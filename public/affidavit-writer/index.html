Malayalam typing is on. Type the name in English (e.g. sajin) and pick one of the Malayalam spellings: click it, use ↑ ↓, or just press Space for the first. To keep the English as typed, pick the Aa row at the bottom of the list or press Ctrl+Enter (that word is then remembered and stays English next time). Letters followed by a digit (KL01) stay English automatically. Numeric fields are not converted.

English form. Type in English. Fill the details on the left; the form text on the right fills in as you type. Words that do not apply (father / mother, he / she, I / We ...) are struck through, and reasons that do not apply get a cross mark.
